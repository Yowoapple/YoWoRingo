100 DaysWorking title

Premise

Two siblings.A debt they never made.One hundred days.

Their parents' problems become theirs. A brother and a sister are left with a debt, and one hundred days to pay it back.

It is a dark story, and it is told through numbers as much as words: every value in the game's systems and every line of its script is tuned by hand, so that each day feels heavier than the last.
