Chapter 01

  1. 2017 — 2024 / Minecraft

    Built a national railwayfor a country thatdoesn't exist.

    On the Ringo MRT Server, a group of friends and I founded the Republic of Ringo, and inside it, the National Ringo Railway Corporation. I built stations, and wired them with redstone and command blocks until minecarts dispatched, stopped and opened platform screen doors on their own.

    • Architecture
    • Redstone
    • Command blocks
    • Seven years

  2. Junior high / Scratch

    Rebuilt Papers, Pleasein Scratch.

    My school sent me to a city-wide competition. I brought a border-checkpoint game inspired by Papers, Please, and came home with a Merit Award from New Taipei City.

    • Game design
    • Merit Award

  3. 2018 — now / TWERG

    "Can I add youon Discord?"

    One question in an earthquake livestream chat turned into the Taiwan Earthquake Recording Group. Eight years later it is a community of 10,000 people. I built its website, its heavy rain warning map and its DYFI intensity algorithm: JavaScript and CSS on the front, Python to train and check the math, Cloudflare to keep it online.

    • Founder
    • 10,000 members
    • JavaScript
    • Python
    • Cloudflare

  4. VRChat / Xiaomi 14 Ultra

    Learned to seein worlds thatdon't exist.

    I could not take a photo until I started shooting inside VRChat. Frame after virtual frame taught me photography by subtraction: take things away until only the point remains. Now I reach for long lenses to find what the naked eye walks past.

    • Composition by subtraction
    • 75mm
    • 120mm
    Enter Focal Length

  5. In development / Unity

    Two siblings.A debt they never made.One hundred days.

    A dark galgame where every number and every line of text is tuned by hand. Planning, programming, writing and systems design are mine. Art, music and sound come from collaborators.

    • Narrative design
    • Systems
    • Unity
